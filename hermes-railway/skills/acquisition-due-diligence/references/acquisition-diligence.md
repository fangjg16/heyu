# Acquisition Diligence

Coordinate three independent workpapers using the shared evidence contract:

- Business: what is sold, to whom, how delivery and cash generation work, owner/key-person dependence, customers, suppliers, people, systems, and operating bottlenecks.
- Industry: demand durability, alternatives, competitive intensity, value-chain power, disruption, regulation, and operating comparables.
- Financial: order-to-cash, revenue quality, normalized owner earnings, margins, working capital, capex, debt-like items, tax, related parties, and quality of earnings.

Add acquisition-specific tests: transferable contracts and relationships, owner replacement cost, required management depth, stranded costs, transition services, hidden capex, and change-of-control consequences. Use a request list for scope and claim audit for evidence verification; neither substitutes for the three conclusions.
