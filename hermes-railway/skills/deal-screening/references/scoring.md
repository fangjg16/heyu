# Dimension Scoring

Use in Phase 4. Score the project on its own merits. Score each dimension independently. Do not compute a total and back-solve the rows.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `meets` | Intake plus public enrichment show this dimension reaches the bar for starting diligence now |
| `partially_meets` | Some support and material reservations: only part of the bar, or weak / unstable evidence |
| `misses` | Available evidence shows the bar is not met |
| unevaluated | Facts required to choose one of the three states are still missing after enrichment |

Unevaluated is not a fourth score. It is a refusal to guess. Missing data is never `misses`. Missing data is never `meets` filled in with industry folklore.

Each scored row must contain: the criterion, the state, evidence with source IDs, the reason, and, if `misses`, the specific cause. If unevaluated, name the missing fact and why it cannot be inferred.

## A. Market timing

**Criterion:** For a financial investor, the category window is open or opening: someone is paying, or public evidence shows payment is forming. The window is not structurally closed, not merely a concept boom, and not a repeat of a publicly failed path without a new mechanism.

Primary input: Wave 1. Correct with Wave 3 demand signals.

`meets` — dated public evidence of paying demand or a forming budget, and no clear sign the window has already closed.

`partially_meets` — mixed signals: growth in an adjacent definition, capital heat without proof of payment, or a window that may be late-cycle.

`misses` — demand is not forming, the window has passed, the category is structurally blocked, or the project repeats a documented failure path with no new mechanism.

Heat in fundraising headlines, by itself, is not `meets`.

## B. Competitive positioning

**Criterion:** Against alternatives customers already use (direct products, traditional methods, in-house build, or doing without), the target has a position that the materials or public facts can support. Category adjectives are not a position.

Primary input: Wave 2. Wave 4 tests whether the claimed customer is reachable.

`meets` — alternatives are named honestly, and a specific, observable difference is consistent with public facts.

`partially_meets` — a possible difference exists but is generic, unproven, or easy to copy; or the field is crowded with only a narrow untested wedge.

`misses` — no supported difference, or the materials deny competitors while Wave 2 shows clear alternatives.

If the materials say there are no competitors and Wave 2 names alternatives, this dimension is not `meets`.

## C. Team / founder quality

**Criterion:** Publicly checkable capability and identity consistency make it believable that this team can execute the critical steps in the materials, with no already-visible integrity red flag.

Look at: relevant, checkable experience; whether co-founders have actually worked together; consistency of public roles; exposed integrity or compliance signals. Titles and vision statements are not evidence.

`meets` — identities resolve, relevant execution evidence exists, no integrity flag after a name-checked search.

`partially_meets` — team can be identified but the relevant operating proof is thin, or key roles are still unnamed.

`misses` — identities do not resolve, claimed history is contradicted, or a name-checked integrity issue is already visible.

A name-only media hit is not a finding. Record material identity, control, affiliation, or adverse-record signals for `due-diligence` Phase 3B. Keep this dimension unevaluated, or score `misses` when the public record is already decisive after identity resolution. Do not open due-diligence during scoring unless the user asked for the deep dive now.

## Readability floor

If intake cannot restate who pays, for what, and how cash arrives, score any dimension that can still be scored. The overall verdict in the memo must not exceed `Watch`. Put the business definition first in open questions. This floor is not a fourth scored dimension.

## Out of scope for scoring

- family-office mandate, sector taste, geography preference, ticket, EBITDA floor, deal type
- valuation, IRR, terms
- full market sizing, financial quality, going-concern legal conclusions
- comparable prior deals and network maps
