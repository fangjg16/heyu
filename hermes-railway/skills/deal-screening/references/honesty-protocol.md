# Honesty Protocol

This skill exists to help the team decide whether to spend diligence time — not to help a deal advance. Softening a thin file into an exciting story wastes the next stage.

## Tell the truth, even when it stops the work

- If the window is closed, say so in the verdict, not in a footnote.
- If the materials contradict public facts, quote both sides.
- Challenge “行业空间巨大”, “没有竞争对手”, and any growth claim with no customer, price, or volume underneath.
- Deal momentum, a warm introduction, and sunk reading time are not evidence.

## Separate facts from claims

- The user’s paragraph and the target deck are claims. They become facts only with independent sources under `references/shared/source-grading.md`.
- Use the four-state claim record in `references/shared/evidence-contract.md` when a statement will travel into diligence.
- Unevaluated plus an open question beats a confident `meets`.
- An estimate is an estimate. Do not present it as a current run-rate.

## Missing data

- Missing data is not `misses`.
- Missing data is not `Pass`.
- `Pass` and `misses` require adverse evidence already in hand.
- When a search finds nothing, write the queries and the coverage limit. A quiet search is not a clean target.

## People

- A name-only match is never a finding.
- Do not moralise past the evidence.
- Escalate identity, control, affiliation, or adverse-record questions to `due-diligence` Phase 3B instead of approximating a legal conclusion.

## Flags

End the memo with Flags:

- **Red** — public hard stops or integrity issues that should halt diligence time now
- **Yellow** — a key dimension unevaluated, or public facts in conflict with the materials

If none, write “No flags identified” and the search coverage that supports that sentence. Flags do not replace the team decision.

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | What it looks like | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Folklore scoring | `meets` on timing because “这个赛道很热” | Cite a dated public fact or leave unevaluated |
| Gap as miss | “没搜到客户” written as `misses` | Unevaluated + open question |
| Mandate smuggling | Scoring geography or ticket taste | Score only timing, position, and team |
| Competitor denial laundering | Accepting “没有竞争对手” after Wave 2 found alternatives | `partially_meets` or `misses`, plus an unreasonable-claim question |
| Mini-diligence | Full TAM triangle, three statements, legal memo | Four waves at screening depth; send the rest to diligence |
| Verdict as pipeline | Writing `declined` into `pipelineStatus` | Draft `Pass`; wait for the team |
| Rounding for the target | Explaining away a contradiction in the target’s favour | Put it in open questions |

## Ground rules

- Every scored dimension needs a criterion, a state, evidence, and a reason it is being tested.
- Deepen only what can change whether diligence starts now.
- Do not present an agent verdict as a committee or pipeline decision.
