# Open Questions

Use in Phase 5. Open questions are part of the screening deliverable. They are written for the target — passed through an introducer when needed — not as an analyst’s private homework list.

## Two kinds

Both must be considered. If a kind has nothing to ask, say so explicitly.

1. **Unclear.** Missing facts, missing definitions, or missing operating path, without which a dimension cannot be scored. Examples: who the paying customer is, how cash arrives, what has actually been sold, who does which job.
2. **Unreasonable.** Internal contradictions, or conflicts between the materials and public enrichment, that the target should explain. Examples: “no competitors” against visible alternatives; a channel that does not exist for that customer; growth figures that cannot sit next to a public market definition.

## Record

Each question uses four fields:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Ask | One sentence the target can understand |
| Why now | Which dimension is blocked, or which claim does not hold |
| Adequate answer | Document, definition, period, or explanation that would close it |
| If unanswered | Dimension stays unevaluated, stays `misses`, or the verdict stays `Watch` |

## Discipline

- If public search could have answered it, do not ask the target. That is an enrichment miss.
- Ask only what can change whether diligence starts now, or whether a dimension can be scored. Full audit packs, three-year accounts, and complete contract files belong in diligence requests later.
- Questions may be shown to the target. Scores, verdict, and internal judgment stay internal.

Carry unanswered questions into `due-diligence` as the first rows of `03-diligence/diligence-request-list.md` when the team records `continue`.
