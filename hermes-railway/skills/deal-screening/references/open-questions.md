# Open Questions

Use in Phase 5. Keep two separate lists in the screening memo: section 7.1 contains questions for the target, and section 7.2 contains internal verification and judgment questions. Target questions may be passed through an introducer when needed; internal analysis stays internal. Creating the memo does not authorize sending it or its questions.

## Target questions — section 7.1

### Two kinds

Both must be considered. If a kind has nothing to ask, say so explicitly.

1. **Unclear.** Missing facts, missing definitions, or missing operating path, without which a dimension cannot be scored. Examples: who the paying customer is, how cash arrives, what has actually been sold, who does which job.
2. **Unreasonable.** Internal contradictions, or conflicts between the materials and public enrichment, that the target should explain. Examples: “no competitors” against visible alternatives; a channel that does not exist for that customer; growth figures that cannot sit next to a public market definition.

### Record

Each question uses four fields:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Ask | One sentence the target can understand |
| Why now | Which dimension is blocked, or which claim does not hold |
| Adequate answer | Document, definition, period, or explanation that would close it |
| If unanswered | Dimension stays unevaluated, stays `misses`, or the verdict stays `Watch` |

### Discipline

- If public search could have answered it, do not ask the target. That is an enrichment miss.
- Ask only what can change whether diligence starts now, or whether a dimension can be scored. Full audit packs, three-year accounts, and complete contract files belong in diligence requests later.
- Questions may be shown to the target. Scores, verdict, and internal judgment stay internal.

## Internal verification and judgment — section 7.2

Record the questions the analyst or investment team still needs to answer independently. Do not repeat the target's material requests or merely list activities.

Each item includes:
- the internal question;
- current view and evidence gap;
- independent verification method or judgment criterion;
- any dependency on a target question, using its Q-ID;
- completion criterion and consequence for the screening decision.

For example, the target may need to explain the basis for a forecast of 100 paying customers. The internal question is whether that forecast is credible given procurement cycles, sales capacity, and independent demand evidence, and whether a lower outcome would still justify further work.

Public research that can be completed in this screening run must be completed before the memo. Retain only unresolved items with a stated access, timing, or evidence limitation, or decisions that require human judgment. State the next verification action, information dependency, milestone to wait for, or reassessment condition. Keep human decisions distinct from the agent's recommendation.

## Handoff

Carry unanswered target questions into the subsequent request list and retain internal verification / judgment items as analyst follow-ups when the team records `continue`. Preserve the distinction between target evidence and independent assessment.
