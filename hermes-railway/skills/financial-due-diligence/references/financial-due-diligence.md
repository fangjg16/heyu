# Financial Due Diligence

Reconcile definitions, entity scope, accounting periods, currencies, and source systems before calculating trends. Test:

- order/contract/delivery/acceptance/invoice/revenue/collection bridges;
- revenue mix, recurrence, concentration, cut-off, cancellations, returns, credits, and non-recurring items;
- gross margin by product/customer/channel and cost classification;
- operating expense normalization and owner/related-party items;
- working capital, cash conversion, debt, restricted cash, commitments, and capex;
- tax, related parties, internal controls, audit adjustments, and quality of earnings;
- forecast bridge from operational drivers to financial results.

State reconciled facts, normalization adjustments, sample limitations, contradictions, data lineage, and residual uncertainty. Trace forecast assumptions to business and industry evidence, then provide diligence-adjusted inputs to returns analysis. Output `financial-diligence.md`.
