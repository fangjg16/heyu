# Acquisition Gate

The final gate decides `Acquire`, `Acquire with conditions`, `Renegotiate`, `Hold`, or `Do not acquire`. Freeze it against named evidence, diligence, financing, and economics versions.

Check durable cash generation, business transferability, market and regulatory durability, normalized financials, financing and liquidity, downside survival, buyer fit, transition, value-creation realism, unresolved critical matters, and conditions. Identify who accepts each exception. The gate does not replace legal, tax, or transaction specialists and does not perform contract review.
