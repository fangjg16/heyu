# Buyer Fit and Transition

Test whether the named buyer can actually take control. Assess relevant skills, credibility with employees/customers/suppliers/lenders, learning curve, time and location constraints, financial resilience, appetite for the operating reality, missing leadership roles, and support network.

Map day 1, first 30 days, first 100 days, and year 1. Preserve continuity before attempting transformation. Identify seller dependence, knowledge transfer, key-person retention, customer reassurance, cash controls, reporting, governance, and early-warning indicators. A value-creation plan is not credible until transition risk is addressed.
