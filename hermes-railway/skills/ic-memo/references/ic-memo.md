# IC Memo

An IC memo is a decision snapshot, not a running notebook. Freeze it against named evidence, diligence, model, risk, and KB versions. Include:

- recommendation and exact decision requested;
- business essence, core capability, industry position, financial quality, and team/governance;
- transaction structure, value, investor cash flows, base/upside/downside, and break-even;
- material risks, mitigations, conditions, stop conditions, and counterarguments;
- unresolved critical matters and their decision consequences;
- attributable human exceptions and responsibility boundaries;
- next action and what would invalidate the recommendation.

Use `Proceed`, `Proceed with conditions`, `Renegotiate`, `Defer`, or `Reject`. A conditional memo must name every condition.
