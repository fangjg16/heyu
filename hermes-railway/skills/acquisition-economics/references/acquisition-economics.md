# Acquisition Economics

Build uses and sources: purchase price, fees, working-capital adjustment, debt-like items, immediate capex, transition cost, buyer equity, senior/deferred/seller debt, earn-out, rollover, and contingency. Model debt service, covenants, liquidity runway, owner compensation, distributions, reinvestment, dilution, and exit.

Report cash-on-cash return, debt-service coverage, leverage, payback, IRR/MOIC when useful, and minimum operating performance needed to avoid distress. Use base, downside, and severe-but-plausible cases. If price or financing is unknown, solve for affordable price/equity ranges. Never treat leverage as free return enhancement.
