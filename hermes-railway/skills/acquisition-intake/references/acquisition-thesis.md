# Acquisition Thesis and Intake

Capture the buyer's transferable skills, operating preferences, geographic and life constraints, capital and financing hypotheses, desired role, sectors to include or exclude, business-quality criteria, and unacceptable dependencies. Unknown budget, leverage, return, and hold period may remain ranges or unknowns.

Translate the thesis into testable criteria rather than a rigid gate. Record why each criterion matters and which evidence would change it. A target can enter evidence collection before every thesis variable is resolved.
