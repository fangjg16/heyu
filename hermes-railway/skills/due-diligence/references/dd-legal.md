# Compliance Check

Use in Phase 3E as the going-concern legal screen. Identify applicable regimes, required permissions, current evidence, uncertainty, operational and economic consequence, and specialist escalation. Feed material findings into business diligence, risk analysis, returns, and conditions precedent. Do not perform contract redlining or general legal-response work.

Scope legal and regulatory work to investment relevance. Identify applicable regime, regulated activity, entity and jurisdiction, permission or duty, current evidence, compliance status, renewal/continuing obligation, uncertainty, and consequence for operations, cash flow, ownership rights, value, or transaction feasibility.

Classify findings as verified compliant, apparent gap, ambiguous applicability, or specialist review required. State practical remediation, timing, cost, gating consequence, and evidence needed. This is not legal advice, contract review, NDA triage, or transaction-document drafting.
