# Business Due Diligence

## Questions

- What is actually sold, to whom, for what job, and through which contract or buying process?
- How do demand generation, sales, delivery, acceptance, billing, collection, service, and renewal connect?
- Which products, technology, data, relationships, licenses, people, or processes are core capabilities rather than descriptions?
- How concentrated and durable are customers, suppliers, channels, and key personnel?
- Which bottlenecks, dependencies, or capacity constraints limit growth?
- What must remain true for the forecast and investment thesis to work?

Use transaction samples and process traces, not management narrative alone. Send revenue/margin/cash reconciliation to financial diligence. Send market demand, alternatives, and competitor tests to industry diligence. Output conclusion, verified facts, claim tests, operating model, core-capability assessment, gaps, implications, and next actions in `business-diligence.md`.
