# Target Screening

Use screening to prioritize diligence, not to manufacture certainty. Assess business clarity, recurrence and concentration, customer/supplier dependence, cash generation, owner dependence, capex and working capital, market durability, transition complexity, asking-price logic, financing plausibility, and buyer fit.

For each criterion record evidence, confidence, open question, and next test. Output `target-screen.md` with `advance`, `advance with priority questions`, `hold`, or `stop`. An `advance` is not an acquisition recommendation.
