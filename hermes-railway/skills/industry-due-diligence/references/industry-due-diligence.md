# Industry Due Diligence

## Research versus diligence

External research is the evidence-acquisition method. Industry diligence is the decision-oriented interpretation of that evidence against this target. The former finds and grades facts; the latter tests whether the target's market story and competitive position are investable.

## Questions

- Is the market definition decision-useful, non-overlapping, and consistent with what the target sells?
- What creates demand, who pays, and what adoption friction or substitution exists?
- How large is the addressable market under transparent top-down and bottom-up definitions?
- Where does value accrue across the chain, and how do bargaining power and structure change?
- How do named competitors and substitutes differ in product, price, distribution, business model, unit economics, retention, and growth?
- Which regulation or technology shifts change the opportunity or continued operation?

When evidence is insufficient, create a specific evidence request and use the public information search method. Include operating comparables here. Put valuation multiples, precedent transaction values, and price implications in returns analysis. Output `industry-diligence.md`.
