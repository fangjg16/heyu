# Deep Reference - Diligence Claim Audit

Use by default for initial/full KB generation. Use whenever materials contain claims about revenue, rights, users, capacity, contracts, regulatory status, partnerships, valuation, or market position.

## Method
1. Extract decision-relevant claims.
2. Bind each claim to evidence, source type, date, and certainty.
3. Identify contradictions, unsupported leaps, stale evidence, and missing originals.
4. Route supported facts to the correct slot.
5. Route unsupported or contradictory claims to diligence-gaps and, if material, risks-mitigation.

## Claim Types
- Ownership/control claims.
- Regulatory/compliance claims.
- Revenue, cost, KPI, customer, or pipeline claims.
- Contract, license, exclusivity, partnership, or channel claims.
- Valuation, exit, return, and financing claims.

## Output Standard
Every major gap should include owner/source needed, urgency, blocker status, why it matters, and next action. Do not let diligence-gaps become a vague shopping list.
