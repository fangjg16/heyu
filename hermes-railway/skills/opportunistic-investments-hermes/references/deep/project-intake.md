# Deep Reference - Project Intake

Use by default for initial/full KB generation and whenever a new project has rough materials, a teaser, deck, email thread, or public-only idea.

## Capture
Extract or mark missing: project name, target/asset, sector, jurisdiction, sponsor/seller/introducer, deal type, what is being bought/financed, current owner/control, proposed capital, use of proceeds, investor economics, expected exit, stage, and materials received.

## Maturity Logic
Factor A = hard-evidence coverage across 13 core slots. Factor B = independent authoring parties. Use evidence caps:
- No project-specific material: cap project-specific slots and make gaps explicit.
- No ownership/entity proof: cap legal-ownership and related risks.
- No investor terms/cash flow: cap valuation-returns.
- No regulatory path: cap regulatory-compliance.

## Seed KB
Create every slot, even if weak. Missing information should become a useful request: what source is needed, who likely owns it, and what decision it unlocks.

## Guided Questions
Prefer 3-7 decision-relevant questions over a long generic questionnaire. Ask about asset being financed, owner/control, investor economics, regulatory gates, project-specific evidence, and date-driven decision nodes.
